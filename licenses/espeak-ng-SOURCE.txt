piper_phonemize.wasm, piper_phonemize.data and piper_phonemize.mjs
====================================================================

These files are the Piper phonemizer compiled to WebAssembly. They include eSpeak NG and its
espeak-ng-data, which are licensed under the GNU General Public License, version 3 or later (see
COPYING in this directory). They run as a separate program: the app starts them with command-line
arguments and reads their output. The rest of Card Reader is MIT-licensed.

They are built by this project from the following exact sources (also listed in VERSIONS here):

  eSpeak NG (Rhasspy fork)     https://github.com/rhasspy/espeak-ng
                               commit 0f65aa301e0d6bae5e172cc74197d32a6182200f
  piper-phonemize (wasm fork)  https://github.com/wide-video/piper-phonemize
                               commit cfff8e52ebaea37c7e953ae2d06b174acb827ac4
  Emscripten 3.1.47            https://github.com/emscripten-core/emsdk
                               commit 37b85e9eaee5be090569d018cca77a15cacc11b7

Build scripts: phonemizer/Dockerfile and phonemizer/build.sh in https://github.com/Tupeco/text-reader.
piper_phonemize.mjs is the build's piper_phonemize.js with one export line appended.
